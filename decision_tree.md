# Daily Reflection Decision Tree

## Step 1: Identify the Issue
Q1: What is your main issue?

- Productivity
- Emotional Stress
- Confusion

---

## If Productivity:
Q2: Did you sleep well?

- Yes → Check distractions
- No → Suggest rest and proper sleep

---

## If Emotional Stress:
Q2: What are you feeling?

- Anxiety → Suggest deep breathing and journaling
- Anger → Suggest taking a break

---

## If Confusion:
Q2: Do you have a clear goal?

- Yes → Break it into smaller tasks
- No → Define a clear goal first
